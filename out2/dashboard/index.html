How the radar works

Implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth factor × breadth factor × maturity factor.

  • Category multiple — a per-category FDV/TVL multiple (Dexs 0.30, Lending 0.28, Derivatives 0.40, RWA 0.35, Chain 0.50, all others 0.15–0.45, unlisted 0.25): what a token in that line of business has traded at, relative to the value locked in it.
  • Growth factor — the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50% … +150%. Rises are rewarded at 0.6 per point, falls punished at 1.0 per point. No change reported counts as flat.
  • Breadth factor — 1 + 0.04 per extra chain, up to six extra chains: a protocol live on more chains reaches more users.
  • Maturity factor — 0.70 + 0.30 × min(age, 3 years) / 3 years: a longer record with no token is a longer record to price. Age unknown counts as one year.

What counts as pre-token. A protocol records no token when DefiLlama’s record carries no market cap, no CoinGecko id and no symbol for it — which is not proof it has none. The board leaves out wrapped, staked and bridged products, centralised exchanges and versioned pools (the watchlist’s rule, without its $50M floor): 40 protocols sit on the watchlist and 138 more below it, for 198 ranked rows.

Farm plans. Every row carries six steps: set up a clean wallet, the protocol’s core action, a second action, a weekly action, checking its own points page, and holding long enough to be counted in more than one snapshot. Each step states what it costs and how long it takes. Never pay to claim anything, and claim only from links the protocol itself publishes.
